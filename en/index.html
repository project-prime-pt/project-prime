What makes Project Prime strong

Less typing, more control.

The app is built to solve everyday fitness problems faster: recognizing food, saving recipes, logging training and keeping goals in view.

✦

AI recognition for food and dishes

Photograph a loose food item or a dish. Project Prime recognizes what's in front of you, checks its own database first, and only creates an estimate when no reliable match exists yet.

▦

Barcode scan

Scan packaging, pull up product data, and reuse it straight from your own database next time.

◷

Live goals

Calories, macros, water, steps and weight are shown in a structured daily view.

Nutrition

Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks under control.

Project Prime shows you what you've eaten today, which meal is still open, and how your day matches up to your goals. Frequent entries and recently used food items can be added again in seconds.

  • Log food by search, photo or barcode
  • Beverages like cola, Fanta or Sprite are handled sensibly as beverages
  • Your own food database with a star marker for reliable matches
  • Use portions, pieces, bars or grams, whichever is available

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Project Prime without premium?

Yes. The app can be used for free, though certain features may be limited by free-plan quotas.

Are my own recipes visible to everyone?

No. Private recipes and saved dishes stay tied to your account, unless you deliberately submit a recipe for community review.

Does Project Prime replace medical advice?

No. The app is for documentation, motivation and orientation. Medical questions belong with doctors or other appropriately qualified professionals.
